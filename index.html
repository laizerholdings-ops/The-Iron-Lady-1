About The Iron Lady

Cape Town's Expert Solar & Window Care Specialists

The Iron Lady All-in-One Services is Cape Town's specialist in professional solar panel cleaning using pure deionized water technology. We help homeowners and businesses maintain their solar installations through safe, effective and environmentally friendly cleaning methods.

Our specialised cleaning approach removes accumulated dust, salt spray from the Atlantic coastline, bird droppings, and environmental deposits that can severely compromise solar panel efficiency.

In addition to solar care, we offer professional window cleaning as our premium secondary service, delivering streak-free, crystal-clear results that brighten your entire property.
